Elements: Borger's wedges
squares

Made exactly as the font build makes Borger's elements (White or Black, the family you are editing). Inserted in the middle of the view as its own contours, selected: drag it into place, then Merge.

Font units: 24 = one of Borger's squares, baseline at Y 0, cap line at 624. Angles count from the horizontal, anticlockwise: ↗ is 45°, ↘ is 135°.

Guide line

Drag the line or its round handle to move it; it clicks onto nodes. Drag the filled handle to rotate: it clicks into every 15°, Shift keeps it to 45° steps.

At reading size

Edits are saved automatically as commits on the branch atelier (White and Black separately); every push builds the fonts (GitHub Actions). Export downloads an SVG.
